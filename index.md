# Privacy Policy for Postiz Lokal

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Postiz Lokal is a private, self-hosted tool used by one person (the owner) to schedule and publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

## 1. What data the app accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, the app asks Google for permission to:

- read the owner's basic Google profile and email address, to identify the connected account;
- upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on the owner's channel;
- read the owner's channel and video information;
- read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel.

## 2. How the data is used

The data is used only to publish and schedule the owner's own videos and to show the owner's own channel information inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## 3. Storage

Account tokens and scheduling data are stored in a database on the owner's own computer. The data is not uploaded to any third-party server operated by the app owner.

## 4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, transfer or share Google user data with any third party.

## 5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Data remains on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored data by removing the channel from the app.
